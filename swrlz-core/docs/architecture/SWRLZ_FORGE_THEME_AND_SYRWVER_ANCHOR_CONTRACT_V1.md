# §wyrlz Forge Theme and §wyrver Anchor Contract v1

## Canonical identity
§wyrlz is the platform and architectural philosophy. §wyrver is the presentation interpreter that maps the active theme onto stable §wyrlz construction anchors. The active anchored theme is **Dragon Master**.

## Immutable construction anchors
Themes may reinterpret appearance, terminology, animation, sound, lore, and Council presentation, but must preserve:
- Truth Firewall distinctions between fact, inference, projection, and recommendation;
- explicit approval gates and user sovereignty;
- Project Conveyor stage semantics;
- evidence, lineage, Chronicle, rollback, and Armory meanings;
- offline-first and local-versus-remote boundaries;
- compatibility identifiers and protocol discipline;
- accessible plain-language equivalents for themed terminology.

## Dragon Master lexicon
- Foundry: project intake.
- Smelting: analysis and normalization.
- Forging: architecture planning and candidate shaping.
- Smithing: approved implementation.
- Tempering: verification and integrity.
- Quenching: snapshot and rollback.
- Inspection: validation.
- Armory: package registry, candidates, releases, and rollback inventory.
- Chronicle: evidence-backed project history.
- Strike: one bounded approved checkpoint.
- Anvil: active project workspace.
- Bellows: processing intensity and battery/concurrency policy.

## Heat declaration
- Cold Forge: read-only observation.
- Warm Forge: documentation, metadata, and configuration.
- Hot Forge: source modification.
- White Forge: major architectural migration.
- Master Forge: release-candidate inspection.

Heat is informational and may never replace exact technical scope, affected paths, risk, rollback, or approval statements.

## Custom themes
Future custom themes may remap terminology and presentation through §wyrver. Activation requires anchor compliance. A theme must provide technical fallback labels and may not hide or weaken approval gates, evidence, lineage, Truth Firewall behavior, or rollback visibility.
